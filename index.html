Download Mise for Windows

Checking for the latest release…

Windows 10 or later · 64-bit · no admin rights needed · unsigned installer

1. Install

Run Mise-Setup. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.

2. Sign in

Your browser opens. Sign in to your Mise account and approve this PC. Sign-up needs an invite code.

3. Work

Open Mise from the Start menu or at misecloud.cloud. Claude Code, Codex, Claude Desktop and Pi connect to Mise automatically.

Good to know

  • Mise runs in the background on your PC and starts when you sign in to Windows. It updates itself when no work is running. Your projects are kept through updates and uninstall.
  • The browser and your AI tools reach your PC through Mise cloud. Mise cloud keeps your account and your linked PCs, not your projects or media. Pause a PC on your account page to stop all access to it.
  • Mise is unsigned. Get it only from this page or the release list, and never turn off Windows security or antivirus.
  • AI generation needs your own provider account or AI tool. Subscriptions, local models and specialist tools are not included. Mise downloads FFmpeg from its publisher on first start.
  • If you have the older Mise desktop app (0.0.13 or earlier), uninstall it first. Your projects are kept.
  • To remove Mise, open Settings › Apps › Installed apps › Mise › Uninstall.
